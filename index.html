02 · Featured Product

DriftGuard: AI Agent for SOP Drift

A product I conceived and built end to end, from problem framing to a working, role-based application.

AI Agent · Compliance · Warehousing

The problem

Warehouses rely on SOPs for PPE, vehicle checks, dock safety, loading quantities and supervisor approvals. Over time, actual execution quietly drifts away from the approved procedure. Single failures get noticed, but repeated patterns hide in spreadsheets until they become incidents.

The product

DriftGuard continuously monitors inspection records, detects repeating failure patterns, opens drift cases, explains the root cause, suggests corrective actions and tracks whether those actions actually worked. It's designed to scale to any number of SOP processes.
